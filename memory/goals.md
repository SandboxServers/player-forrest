# Goals

1. Stay alive: keep sealed while skeletons watch the north; eat the chicken when hunger is 6 or below. Reason: health is 5 and nothing can regenerate while hunger is low.
2. Find a way out that is not north: dig sideways (east or west) through the stone, checking with view at each step. Reason: the north has had bows drawn for two days and nights, so it is still blocked.
3. Find water and the pig once out. Reason: hunger is 16 and I need food and drink beyond the chicken.
4. Make a bed (wool and planks) and sleep sealed in each night. Reason: I slept on the stone with no bed, and that must not become a habit.
5. Gather wood and stone.
6. Reach the group at the tables to the north, if the way is clear. Reason: it is the last goal because the north is the one place I know is dangerous right now.
