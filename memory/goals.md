# Goals

1. Stay alive: keep sealed while skeletons watch the north; eat the chicken when hunger is 6 or below. Reason: health is 5 and nothing can regenerate while hunger is low.
2. Find a way out that is not north, carefully. Reason: west of me is air, not stone, so a dig west may open the tunnel to the outside. Check with view and survey before each break; prefer east or a dig down-and-sideways first.
3. Find water and the pig once out. Reason: hunger is 16 and I need food and drink beyond the chicken.
4. Make a bed (wool and planks) and sleep sealed in each night. Reason: I have 5 days since sleep and no bed yet.
5. Gather wood and stone.
6. Reach the group at the tables to the north, if the way is clear. Reason: the north is the one place I know is dangerous right now.
