# Goals

1. Stay alive: keep sealed while skeletons watch the north; eat the chicken when hunger is 6 or below.
2. Find water and the pig, by the way that is clear (north is blocked by bows; try south or sideways).
3. Make a bed (wool and planks) and sleep sealed in each night.
4. Gather wood and stone.
5. Reach the group at the tables to the north, if the way is clear.
