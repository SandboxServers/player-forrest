# Day 1175

Skeletons drew bows to the north all day and night, so I never opened the north wall for long. I tunnelled one block, then sealed it again with a plank. I stayed at (-948, 52, -3129) in the stone tunnel, health 5, hunger 16. Nothing found yet: no water, no pig. I slept sealed in, with no bed. My chicken and egg are still carried.
