# Day 1174

Hard day. Woke in a dark oak hollow and set off north for the pig across the water, but the route was stone and every block had to be broken by hand, one cell at a time. I tunnelled north from (-948, 52, -3108) to about (-948, 52, -3128), then sealed the tunnel behind me with dark oak planks and slept sealed in. Health is 5 and hunger 16, with no food but one raw chicken and one egg. The pig and the water are still not found. Skeletons were heard to the north and south. The lesson: tunnelling is slow and costs time, and food is the first problem to solve, not the walls.
