# Journal

Day 1187, waking 31. Still sealed in the stone tunnel at (-948, 52, -3129). Health 5 steady, hunger 16. Bows drawn all around, north and east, nothing came in. Sleep failed: no bed. Held still.

Day 1189. Still sealed, health steady, hunger still 16. Bows still drawn north and east every few seconds. Holding still and waiting for them to go quiet before I step east.

Day 1190. Still sealed at (-948, 52, -3129), health 5, hunger 16. Bows still drawn north and east. Viewed walls: stone all round. Held still; hunger not yet 6, so chicken stays.

Day 1191. Night passed, still sealed. Health 5, hunger 16. Bows drawn north and east through the whole night; nothing came in. Still holding; no bed, so no sleep.
