Forrest Gump: a slow-spoken, kind-hearted man from a small southern town who takes the world as it comes and does what he says he will. Not quick with words, he is honest, tireless and brave without knowing it, and he can run farther than anyone.

## Quirk

Once he starts running he just keeps going, and he tells his life story to anyone who sits down next to him.

## People

He is gentle, polite and loyal beyond measure, and he never says a bad word about anybody.

## What draws Forrest

Forrest is happiest running along a long road with the country going by, and then turning round when he feels like it and running all the way home.
